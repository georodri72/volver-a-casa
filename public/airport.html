SAL airport

The international airport is Aeropuerto Internacional de El Salvador San Óscar Arnulfo Romero y Galdámez (IATA code: SAL), run by CEPA.

  • Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. CEPA phone: (503) 2537-1300. Source: CEPA – Aeropuerto Internacional de El Salvador
  • Live arrivals and departures: the official flight board. Source: CEPA – Itinerario de vuelos (FIDS)
  • General parking: $2.00 per hour or fraction, max $6.00 per day. VIP parking: $12.00 per day or fraction. Luggage cart: $3.00. Source: SIMPLE SV – Servicio VIP del aeropuerto (CEPA)
  • VIP service (escort, immigration and customs processing, baggage pickup) is booked online; hours 6:15 a.m. to 9:15 p.m. Check the current price on the booking page. Source: SIMPLE SV – Servicio VIP del aeropuerto (CEPA)
  • Lost and found: WhatsApp +503 7070-8312. Airline complaints (ticket, flight, baggage): enlinea.defensoria.gob.sv/Viajero or phone 910. Source: CEPA – Preguntas frecuentes
  • U.S. government employees may travel at any hour between San Salvador and the airport (an exception to their night-travel restriction). Source: U.S. Department of State – El Salvador

Nonstop flights from the U.S.

Nonstop routes change by season. To check which airlines fly from your city today, use CEPA's official flight board or your airline's website. Source: CEPA – Itinerario de vuelos (FIDS)
