Buying tickets to El Salvador

Airlines, baggage, holidays and booking advice, with sources.

Which airlines fly Washington DC to San Salvador

  • Washington Dulles (IAD) lists San Salvador as a year-round nonstop destination. Source: Washington Dulles (MWAA) – Nonstop destinations
  • El Salvador airport's official flight board showed Avianca flights to Washington when we checked (Oct 18, 2026). The board only shows that day's flights. Source: CEPA – Itinerario de vuelos (FIDS)
  • For Reagan National (DCA), check the airport's nonstop destination list. Source: Reagan National (MWAA) – Nonstop destinations
  • Airlines that appear in DC–SAL fares we saw recently (may include connections): American, Avianca, Copa, Frontier, United. Source: the Aviasales data on this site.
  • Other airlines may fly the route seasonally. Always confirm on the airline's website.

Baggage

Bag rules and fees depend on the airline, fare type and purchase date, so we don't copy amounts here. Check your airline's official page: American, Avianca, Copa, Frontier, United.

On Aviasales, if a fare shows no baggage option, the seller supplied a no-bag fare and you can add bags when booking on the seller's site. Source: Aviasales – How our service works

Dates to plan around (holidays in El Salvador)

  • Article 190 of the Labor Code lists these paid holidays: January 1; Holy Thursday, Friday and Saturday; May 1; August 6; September 15; November 2; December 25. In the city of San Salvador also August 3 and 5 (fiestas agostinas). Later decrees may add other holidays. Source: Código de Trabajo de El Salvador, Art. 190 (Corte Suprema de Justicia)
  • Holy Week 2027: Thursday March 25, Friday March 26 and Saturday March 27, 2027 (Easter Sunday: March 28).
  • We don't have enough history yet to say whether fares rise around these dates. See Cheapest dates for seen prices by date.

When to book

  • According to Expedia's 2026 Air Hacks report (averages across all international economy flights booked from the U.S., not specific to El Salvador), booking 31–45 days ahead saved $190 on average versus booking 6+ months out, and booking 8–15 days ahead saved $225 on average. Source: Expedia – 2026 Air Hacks report
  • Cheap fares sell out fast: if you find a price that works, compare the final total on the seller's site before you decide.
