How our prices work

Plain answers about the fares we show.

Where the prices come from

Prices come from the Aviasales Data API via Travelpayouts. They are prices other travelers saw when they searched for flights on Aviasales, stored in a cache. We don't run live searches or sell tickets.

Which airports we cover

For now we show fares to San Salvador (SAL) only from Reagan National (DCA) and Washington Dulles (IAD). More cities coming soon.

Only the last 6 hours

We show a fare only if we can confirm someone saw it in the last 6 hours and the flight leaves more than 48 hours from now. We use a short window because cheap fares often sell out within hours: a price seen a day ago may already be gone. If an airport has no such fare, you'll see "No recent fares". We check for new data every hour, and the page re-checks each fare's age every time you open it.

Prices are not guaranteed

Cheap seats sell out fast. Today's price can be much higher than the recently seen one. That's why we label it "Price when seen" and the button says "Check today's price": it opens a live search on Aviasales. Prices are per 1 adult, round trip, economy, in US dollars.

Taxes and fees

Travelpayouts' API documentation doesn't say whether these prices include all taxes and fees, so we don't promise that they do. Bags may cost extra depending on the fare. The final total is shown on the seller's site before you pay. Source: Travelpayouts – Aviasales Data API documentation, Aviasales – How our service works

Cheapest-dates calendar

The Cheapest dates page uses the same source but shows the cheapest price seen per departure date over the last 7 days (how long the API keeps data). Each price shows the date it was seen. They are older than the 6-hour fares on the home page: use them to compare dates. We also save one daily snapshot so that, once there is enough history, we can show which months have been cheapest.

Affiliate links

The buttons are Travelpayouts/Aviasales affiliate links. If you book after clicking, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It doesn't change which fares we show.

We never invent prices

Every flight price on this site comes from the API. We don't use sample or estimated prices. The only calculated figure will be the median of seen prices in the history section, labeled as such. If there's no recent data, we say so.
